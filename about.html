ABOUT MAGIC TOUCH GLOBAL

Bringing Professional Services And Practical Solutions Together.

We recognise that individuals, families and organisations have different needs. Finding dependable support for everyday responsibilities and business operations can require time, coordination and the right service provider.

MagicTouch Global Limited brings a range of professional and lifestyle support services together through one company. Our service areas cover recruitment and staffing, household support, caregiving, hospitality, property services, facility management and more.

Our approach is built around understanding each request, communicating clearly and discussing suitable service arrangements based on the client's requirements.
