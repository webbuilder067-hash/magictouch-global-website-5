WHO WE ARE

Introducing MagicTouch Global Limited

MagicTouch Global Limited is a staffing and recruitment company focused on connecting individuals, households and businesses with suitable personnel.

We recognise that finding the right people is important to the smooth running of homes and organisations. Our approach is centred on understanding client needs and supporting the recruitment process with professionalism and care.

Through our services, we aim to create meaningful connections between clients seeking assistance and individuals looking for suitable work opportunities.
